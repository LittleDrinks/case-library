**Publication:** Small Garden Water, edition 1 (2025)<br>

## Before the walk

Choose a planting bed and a nearby path edge. Visit both after the same light shower, and use the same observation interval.

## Record what is visible

1. Note the time and location.
2. Describe changes in soil color and any visible shallow channel.
3. Separate direct observations from explanations that still need checking.

The sample garden image is a generated illustration for this demonstration and does not document a real place.

> “The path edge is darker than the bed after the shower.”
